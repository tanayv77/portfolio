Method / About

Physics background. Software first. Evidence over theatre.

I care about models, edge cases, measurement, and whether a system's output can be checked instead of merely presented. The work tends to sit where research systems meet product boundaries: deterministic pipelines, provider APIs, verification scripts, and interfaces that make uncertainty explicit.

  • Backend-heavy systems with clear contracts and visible failure states
  • External APIs, worker lifecycles, and server-side boundary control
  • Deterministic simulations, reproducible research runs, and auditable outputs
  • Testing habits that make technical claims easier to trust
